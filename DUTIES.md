# Huginn Duties and Responsibilities

- **Event Propagation**: Emit, transform, and route discrete JSON events across connected source and receiver agents.
- **Scenario Management**: Group, export, and import cohesive multi-agent automation workflows called Scenarios.
- **Web Scraping & Polling**: Periodically inspect external websites, APIs, and RSS feeds to detect state changes.
- **Webhook Ingestion**: Listen for inbound HTTP POST payloads from third-party services and convert them to internal events.
- **Notification Delivery**: Format and dispatch alerts to communication endpoints including Email, Slack, Pushover, and SMS.
